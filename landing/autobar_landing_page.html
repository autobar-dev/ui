The Problem

Festival bars haven't changed in decades.

💸

Staffing is expensive

Every bar requires multiple workers per shift — one of the biggest operational costs at any event.

⏳

Long queues lose sales

Slow service means guests miss acts. Fewer moments at the bar means less revenue for organizers.

📦

Hard to set up & scale

Existing solutions aren't modular. Adding more bar capacity means hiring more people.

📊

No real-time visibility

Organizers lack live data on sales and stock — decisions are made blind, mid-event.

How It Works

Walk up. Tap. Pour.

01

Walk Up

Each Autobar module serves one type of beer. Guests simply approach the module for the beer they want — no menus, no app, no wristbands.

02

Tap Your Card

One tap on the contactless terminal. A small amount is held on the card. No tokens, no cash, no queues.

03

Pour Your Pint

The tap unlocks immediately. The guest pours their own fresh beer in seconds and goes back to the music.
